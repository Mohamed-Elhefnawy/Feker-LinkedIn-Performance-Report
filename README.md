# Feker LinkedIn Performance Report

A performance report for a company LinkedIn page, built in Power BI. It covers the page's own results, a comparison with competitor pages, and who the followers are.

**Power BI file:** `Feker LinkedIn Performance Report.pbix`
(An earlier online copy of the report was unavailable when checked.)

## What the report covers

- **Page performance:** new followers, engagement, impressions and clicks.
- **Competitor comparison:** new followers, total followers, posts and engagement by company.
- **Follower demographics:** followers by country, industry, seniority, job function and company size.

## Key findings

- The page gained 190 new followers, 8.03% of its total followers, with spikes on 7 Aug, 24 Aug and 29 Sep.
- Posts got 113 likes, 13 comments and 12 reposts, with an average engagement rate of 6.7%. Engagement spikes faded from early September.
- Posts got 7,871 impressions and 482 clicks, an average click-through rate (CTR) of 6.1%, with one CTR spike on 3 Aug.
- Compared with competitors, the page was well behind on posts published, engagement, new followers and total followers.
- Most followers are in Saudi Arabia, work in IT Services and IT Consulting, hold Media and Communication or Human Resources roles, and work at companies of 11 to 200 employees.

## Recommendations (from the original report)

- Find out what caused the spikes in followers, engagement, impressions and clicks, and repeat it.
- The original report notes that results were organic and recommends targeted campaigns and sponsored posts to reach more people.
- Post more often, since the page published far less than its competitors.
- Aim part of the content at the main follower groups above.
